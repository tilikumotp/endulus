# App Architecture Notes (Person 4, `app/main.py` — v1 Final)

Status: Production v1 — runs 100% offline/locally on verified orbital datasets and visual/3D assets in `data/processed/` (`features_moon.parquet`, `features_earth.parquet`, `moon_atlas/`, `moon_3d/`, `earth_atlas/`, `earth_3d_grids.npz`).

## Feature Summary
- **Core Scoring & Decision #30 Alignment:** All scores come from leader's `src/scoring/` (`axis_scores_with_draws` → `axis_pivot` → `combine_axes_with_uncertainty` → `expanded_interval`; `axis_gaps`; `select_portfolio`; `run_blind_validation`). Under **Decision #30 (`Karar #30`)**, Moon `temp_amplitude` and `thermal_inertia` are `NaN`, so v1 numeric scoring runs on the **2 active numeric axes (`Mobility + Surface`)** while `Thermal` is explicitly documented as suspended (`NaN`).
- **Map (`🗺️ Harita`):** Two-layer PyDeck `ScatterplotLayer` where both validation sites (`role == "validation"`, blue outline, `color_val`) and candidate sites (`color`) encode the mission-weighted similarity score in dot colour and radius.
- **Visual Comparison & Axes (`📊 Görsel Karşılaştırma & Eksenler`):** 2D Physical Feature Space scatter (`slope_mean` vs `albedo` for 40 Earth sites + Moon reference ⭐) and horizontal per-axis bar cards (`Mobility`, `Surface`, `Overall`) with `expanded_interval` uncertainty error bars.
- **3D Terrain (`⛰️ 3D Arazi`):** `app/terrain.py` loads committed `5 m/px` LRO LOLA (`data/processed/moon_3d/{site_id}_3d.npz`, `elev_m`) and `30 m/px` Copernicus GLO-30 / NASADEM (`data/processed/earth_3d_grids.npz`) elevation grids (`is_stub=False` across all 5 Moon and 40 Earth sites).
- **Score Table & Compact Portfolio Card (`📊 Skor Tablosu & Portföy`):** Filterable 40-site score table (`Overall`, `Mobility`, `Surface` with `expanded_interval` bounds; `Thermal` omitted with Decision #30 note) followed immediately by the **Compact Portfolio Card** (`select_portfolio` at `0.70` heuristic threshold with explicit caveat).
- **Descriptive Rank Diagnostic (`✅ Betimsel Sıra Tanısı`):** 10-row single table combining raw blind ranks (`/40` on `Mobility` and `Surface`, with `Thermal` marked `Suspended / NaN`) and `docs/validation_expectations.md` pre-registered expected axes, E8 qualitative ice/permafrost inventory, rationales, and verified DOIs/URLs.
- **Limits of the Analogy (`⚠️ Analojinin Sınırları`):** Physical & technical limits (`5 m` vs `30 m` DEM scale note, Decision #30 thermal note), 8-Dimension Challenge Scorecard, committed local orbital visual evidence card (`LRO LOLA 5 m/px` + `Sentinel-2 L2A 10 m/px` with `collection`, `scene_id`, `acquisition_date`, `local_cloud_pct`), and standardized feature-gap table (`axis_gaps`).
